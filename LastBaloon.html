How much air is too much air?
★ Hold the pump · don't be the pop ★
A backyard party of poor decisions · 1–4 players

SETUP

Players
2
SOLO — YOU vs 1 BOT. The bot picks its own personality!

HOW TO PLAY

1
Take turns adding air

Press and HOLD the INFLATE button. The longer you hold, the bigger the pump. RELEASE to stop — that ends your turn. A quick tap still counts as a small pump.

2
Watch the pressure meter

Green is safe. Yellow is a gamble. Red is… well. Nobody knows exactly where the balloon gives up — it's different every game.

3
Listen to the balloon

The meter exaggerates, but the balloon doesn't. The closer it is to its true limit, the more it creaks, sweats and panics. Keep your ears open.

4
Whoever pops it, loses

Everyone else wins. No rush — take all the time you need for your one big hold. Greed is funny — right up until it isn't.

5
The screen rotates between turns

Each player owns a side of the device: players 1 & 3 see it one way, players 2 & 4 the other. Between turns the whole screen smoothly rotates around, so it's right side up for the next player — just slide the device across the table.

SETTINGS

Rotate the screen between players (1&3 vs 2&4). Turn off if your group shares one view.
