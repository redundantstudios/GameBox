How to play
The basics
Add 2 to 10 players, then tap the bottle to spin. The gold tip is the pointer — whoever it rests on is up. Dashed lines show each player's zone so there is never a doubt.
The round (Mild / Spicy / Fire)
1. The chosen player picks TRUTH (answer honestly) or DARE (perform it live).
2. Got another prompt instead? Tap GIVE ME ANOTHER — free, unlimited.
3. When done, tap DONE · VOTE.
4. Every other player votes in turn — GG means worthy, FLOP means not. The verdict lands only after the last ballot.
5. Majority GG → next spin. FLOP or a tie → the same player picks truth or dare again.
Freestyle
No prompts, no voting. The bottle only names a player — the group invents the question or the task themselves. The name shows on the table with tap to spin again right on the bottle.

Game length
QUICK = 10 rounds, PARTY = 20 rounds, ENDLESS = no limit. When the target hits, the show ends with awards: MVP (most GGs), Serial Flopper, Drama Queen (most dares), Facts Only (most truths) — or Most Picked in Freestyle.
Good to know
Theme, sound and haptics live in the gear icon — reachable anytime, even mid-vote. Freestyle never shows cards or votes. Nothing in the core game is ever locked — Spicy and Fire are extra content, nothing more.
