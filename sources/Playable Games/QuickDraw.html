HOW TO PLAY
1

Add 3 to 10 players, pick rounds, words difficulty, categories and timer, then start.

2

Pass the phone to the named drawer. Everyone else, look away!

3

The drawer sees the secret word once. It cannot be seen again. They draw from memory, and only the category is shown while drawing.

4

Everyone shouts guesses out loud. No typing. The round runs the full timer. Guessed it? The drawer taps CORRECT! and picks who said it first. Faster guesses earn a time bonus for both the guesser and the drawer. Undo, redo, eraser and the fill bucket are all there.

5

If time runs out, nobody scores, not even the drawer. Watch the scoreboard rows slide to their new ranks. Most points after all rounds wins.
