HOW TO PLAY

1

ADD YOUR CREW. Add 1–4 players. Turn seats 2–4 into bots with the robot button and choose player colors.

2

BEAT THE CHALLENGE. Complete the quick mini-game to unlock the pass. Every challenge has its own objective and hint.

3

SOLO. In 1P, finish a challenge to reset the timer and start the next round. Use your free hint or skip when you need it.

4

MULTIPLAYER. The bomb moves from player to player. After the pass animation, hand the device to the next player.

5

FACE EACH OTHER. The screen flips for the next human player, so players sitting opposite each other can play comfortably.

6

WATCH THE TIMER. The fuse keeps counting down. Failing challenges can cost time, so move quickly.

7

DON’T GET CAUGHT. When the fuse reaches 0:00, the player holding the bomb loses and the game ends.
