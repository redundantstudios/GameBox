HOW TO PLAY
1

Everyone secretly gets a word. All civilians share the same word. The Impostors get a different but similar one.

2

The table shows the clue order. In that order, each player says one clue word out loud. Then discuss freely.

3

Tap START VOTING. Every player votes. With 2 impostors, each player selects 2 suspects.

4

The most-voted players are eliminated and revealed. If every impostor is eliminated, Civilians win. If any impostor survives, Impostors win. A tie eliminates nobody: discuss and vote again.
