How to play

  • Take turns putting your stones on empty points.
  • Three of your stones on one line is a mill. A mill lets you remove one enemy stone.
  • A stone standing in an enemy mill cannot be taken, unless there is nothing else to take.
  • When all stones are placed, slide one stone along a line to a free point each turn.
  • A stone that keeps reopening a mill can capture again and again.
  • Win by leaving your rival with two stones, or with no move at all.
