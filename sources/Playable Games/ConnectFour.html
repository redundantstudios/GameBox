HOW TO PLAY

DROP: tap a column to release your marble. Drag to aim, release to drop.

STACK: marbles travel behind the board and settle into the lowest open hole. A full column wobbles, so pick another.

WIN: connect four in a row, across, up and down, or diagonal.

MATCH: face Oakley the Bot at three skill levels, or pass and play as Blue Marble vs Amber Marble.
